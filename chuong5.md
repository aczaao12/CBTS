**5.1.3 The methods of production fish sauce**
Depending on regional weather conditions and brewing customs, each will have its own method of producing fish sauce: 1) Brewing and pressing method; 2) Sun drying and stirring; 3) Mixed method (pressing - sun drying - stirring - brewing) 

**5.1.3.1 Brewing and pressing method**

* Not add the water, not stir, adding salt in one time, pressing 


* Applied in the provinces of South of Vietnam 



**5.1.3.2 Sun drying and stirring**

* Adding the water, adding salt in many times and stirring 


* Applied in the provinces of North of Vietnam
